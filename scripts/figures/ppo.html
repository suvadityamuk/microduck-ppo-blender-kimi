PPO: collect, then improve
1 · ActThe actor network walks 4096 ducks for 24 steps each
2 · ScoreEach step earns a reward: follow the command, don't fall
3 · JudgeThe critic predicts the reward. Actual minus predicted is the advantage
4 · UpdateMake better-than-expected actions more likely, worse ones less
The clip keeps updates small
Across: how much more or less likely an update makes an action. Past ±20 % the gain goes flat, so one batch can't push the policy far from a walk that already works.
